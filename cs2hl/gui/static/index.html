How it works

      
    
        
  1. Find: the demo (replay) is analysed for rounds where you got enough kills or won a clutch.
    2. 
        
  2. Record: CS2 opens by itself, jumps to each highlight, spectates you and saves every frame at a fixed frame rate. This is not screen recording, so the result is perfectly smooth even if your PC stutters. Don't click into the CS2 window while it works; it closes when done.
    3. 
        
  3. Encode: the frames become one MP4 per highlight, plus a montage of the whole match.

Queue

      
Tick several matches (or demos) and press + Add to queue in the floating bar, or click Add to queue on a single match. They render one after another, each producing its clips and its own montage. On the Queue page you can reorder (↑ ↓), remove (✕) or pause. The queue is saved: if you close the app while it is working, it keeps going in the background; if the app is restarted, unfinished items wait until you press Start.

      
What gets clipped

      
Rounds (Settings → What counts as a highlight): tick 3 kills, 4 kills, Ace and/or Won clutches. The whole round is recorded, with long pauses cut out. An ace means the whole enemy team, so it is 5 kills in Premier/Competitive and fewer in Wingman and Rush.

      
Special shots are single kills that get their own short clip even when the round had nothing else: flicks, sniper flicks, spray transfers, long range kills, no-scopes, wallbangs, kills through smoke, jump shots and blind kills. Open the dropdown and tick what you want, or Select all. Flicks are measured from the demo: how far your crosshair moved in the fifth of a second before the first hit.

      
Video styles

      
Smooth · 120 fps: every output frame is a real game frame. Crisp, smooth, and can be slowed down 2× in an editor without stutter.

      
Motion blur · 60 fps: CS2 renders 4× more frames (240 per second), and each group of 4 is averaged into one. Fast movement smears naturally like a real camera, the look of most frag movies. It takes about twice as long.

      
Resolution and graphics

      
Record CS2 at 1080p or 1440p. With 1440p you can save the final video at 1440p, or at 1080p: scaling down from 1440p makes a noticeably sharper 1080p video. 1440p needs a screen of at least 2560 × 1440 and takes longer.

      
Graphics while recording. "Very High" switches CS2 to Valve's Very High preset (high shaders, textures, particles, shadows, ambient occlusion, FSR off; CMAA2 anti-aliasing, because MSAA breaks the colours of recorded frames) just for the recording. Your own settings, display mode and resolution are put back as soon as CS2 closes, so you keep your low settings for playing.

      
Encoder

      
Automatic uses your NVIDIA card (NVENC) if you have one, AMD/Intel otherwise (VAAPI on Linux, AMF or Quick Sync on Windows), and the CPU as a fallback. GPU encoding is tested on an RTX 3070 only; other GPUs are a work in progress. Encoding takes seconds per clip; most of the time goes into CS2 drawing every frame.

      
How long does it take?

      
CS2 saves roughly 10–15 frames per second while recording, so a match with four highlights takes about 15 minutes (Smooth) or 30 minutes (Motion blur). The progress bar shows the time left.

      
Where do matches come from?

      
Steam matches are split by mode (Ranked Competitive, Premier, Rush, Wingman, …) and come from your Steam "Personal Game Data" pages, the same ones with the Download Replay buttons. Replays are downloaded from Valve's replay servers. Those links are plain HTTP, which is why browsers call them unsafe; the files come straight from Valve. Valve deletes replays after a few weeks, so old matches show as expired.

      
Demos on this PC are .dem (or still-compressed .dem.bz2) files in your CS2 replays folder (in-game downloads, or any demo you copy there).

      
Already downloaded it yourself? Use Open on Steam ↗ above the match list to get to the Steam page with the Download Replay buttons. Put the file in the CS2 replays folder (or leave it in your Downloads folder); when you queue that match, the app uses your copy instead of downloading it again, and never deletes it. Matches with a local copy show 💾 on this PC.

      
Troubleshooting

      

        
"The recording plugin did not start inside CS2"
A CS2 update changed something the plugin depends on. Update this app: on Linux git pull then ./install.sh, on Windows download the newest release.

        
CS2 opens but nothing happens / the game stays open
Use Cancel, then try again. If it keeps happening, run cs2hl clean in a terminal to remove the plugin from CS2 (on Windows, the next recording does the same clean-up).

        
"You are not in this demo"
Only your own matches can be clipped. If you play on an account that never logged in on this PC, add its SteamID64 in Settings → Accounts.

        
"Not enough disk space"
Frames are stored temporarily in the workspace folder. Free space there, or reinstall with the workspace on a bigger disk.

        
Encoder errors
The app already falls back to the CPU when a GPU encoder fails. If it still fails, set Encoder to CPU in Settings.

        
CS2 looks different after recording (windowed, low resolution)
The app restores your video settings when CS2 closes. If CS2 crashed, run cs2hl clean (or start any new recording) and they are put back.

        
Steam login expired
Log in again on the Steam account page.
